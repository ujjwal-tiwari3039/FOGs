## 🧠 Our Problem Statement

We were given **ED-05 — Bias-Resistant Short-Answer Assessment**.

The goal was to build a **three-class short-answer assessment model** that focuses on the actual meaning of an answer rather than superficial writing patterns or repeated keywords.

The model was expected to:

- Classify answers into **Correct, Contradictory, and Incorrect**
- Provide **class probabilities**
- Generate **local counterfactuals**
- Measure **robustness and calibration**
- Provide **evidence or explanations** for selected predictions
- Remain stable when the writing style changes without changing the actual meaning
- Avoid giving artificially higher scores simply because important keywords were repeated

In short, the challenge was not just to make a classifier that gets the answer right, but to make one that understands **why** an answer is right.
